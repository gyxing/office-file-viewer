# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in `office-file-viewer` a respectful, constructive, and inclusive experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and receive technical feedback constructively.
- Focus on the problem, evidence, and user impact.
- Respect privacy and do not share confidential documents or personal data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and sharing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this code. Reports should be made privately through the contact method described in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) when the matter involves safety or privacy.

This code applies to project spaces and public interactions representing the project.
